McFarlane's Shelf

The cards had gum; these came with a hockey stick you'd lose in a week. A digital shelf of my personal McFarlane Sports Picks collection, from the first series on.

I started collecting McFarlane'e Sports Picks figures in 2000, when the series only had an NHLPA license, in what is now known known as the "NHLPA" series. McFarlane then secured an NHL license and launched "NHL Series 1" in 2001, which also included all the original "NHLPA" figures repainted in their NHL uniforms as chase variants. McFarlane continued to produce the NHL Sports Picks line until 2014, when it went on a 9 year hiatus. It has since been revived, but I have not really been interested in collecting the new series. I sold my entire collection in 2026, but it is preserved here with some image processing trickery to remove any of the yellowing plastic which plagues the older packaging. Now they look as good as the day I bought them
