McFarlane's Shelf

The cards had gum; these came with a hockey stick you'd lose in a week. A shelf of McFarlane Sportspicks figures, from the first series on.

I started collecting Todd McFarlane's Sportspicks NHL the first year they were released, when the series only had an NHLPA license, known as the "NHLPA" series. McFarlane then secured an NHL release and launched "Series 1" in 2001, and included all the original "NHLPA" figures repainted in their NHL uniforms as chase figures. McFarlane continued to produce the NHL Sports Picks line until 2011, when it went on a 5 year hiatus.
